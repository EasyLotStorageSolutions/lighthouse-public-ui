Opening-day launch layer

Open what is ready. Keep the guardrails on.

Lawyer review has been received for the current opening-day scope. Lighthouse can run Work, Marketplace, Maker Space, Storage, Services, Studio, and Channel as coordination, intake, review, and matching experiences while user-to-user payments, automated hiring, auctions, and regulated store controls remain off.

WHAT IS OPENWHAT STAYS OFF
Core payment rule

Lighthouse does not move money between users.

Businesses, employers, buyers, sellers, makers, workers, storage hosts, and service providers pay each other directly or through their own approved checkout/invoice path. Lighthouse may coordinate the request, but it does not hold funds, split payouts, process payroll, take a commission, or act as escrow.

User-to-user payments OFF

Work

Workers can create Work Passports. Employers can post hiring needs. Matching is permission-based and nonbinding. The employer remains the employer.

Open for controlled matching

Marketplace

People can browse reviewed listings and submit items for review. Storage listings and general Marketplace listings stay separate.

Open with review gates

Maker Space

Makers can apply and buyers can submit custom requests. Maker Space is free during launch. Customers pay makers directly.

Open and free for launch

Storage Network

Storage customers can request Easy Lot storage. Other facilities can request advertising/network review with photos and facility details.

Open with owner review

Services

Support requests can be submitted for reviewed provider routing. Qualifications, pricing, availability, and acceptance remain human-confirmed.

Open with provider review

Store Intelligence

The Store Desk is online for synthetic pilot review only. Real store operations wait for store consent and approved activation.

Review-only pilot
Held switches

These stay off for opening day.

  • User-to-user payments, escrow, payouts, commissions, and payment custody.
  • Auction submissions, bidding, winner settlement, and automatic contracts.
  • Automatic hiring, rejection, worker discipline, payroll, or employer-of-record behavior.
  • External job syndication and AI ranking enhancements for Work.
  • POS writes, fuel control, lottery control, age-verification decisions, auto-ordering, and store equipment control.
  • Provider auto-activation, auto-dispatch, and automatic external messaging.
Opening-day legal status

Lawyer review received for launch scope.

  • Terms of Use and Privacy Policy remain the operating boundary for profile, job, listing, request, facility, Studio, and Channel intake data.
  • Employment boundary remains active: Lighthouse coordinates introductions; employers make hiring, classification, payroll, tax, and compliance decisions.
  • Marketplace boundary remains active: sellers, makers, buyers, and hosts arrange payment and fulfillment directly.
  • Truthful-claims rule remains active: no guaranteed job, hire, earnings, sale, storage availability, provider response, legal compliance, or real-time inventory claim.
  • Nondiscrimination, accessibility, accommodation, and objective hiring criteria language remain active for Work.
  • Worldwide expansion rule remains active: launch broad product wording, but review local labor, privacy, consumer, tax, and marketplace laws before active regional scaling.
Opening-day position: Lighthouse is open for public use within the approved launch scope as a coordination, matching, review, and intake platform. It is not opening as a payment processor, payroll company, employer, auction settlement platform, legal/compliance certifier, or store-control system.
This review center is an operating summary for Steven after lawyer review. It does not replace ongoing legal guidance or activate held systems by itself.
