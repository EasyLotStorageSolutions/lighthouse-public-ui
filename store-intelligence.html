Store Desk without guessing.

A truth-based operating network for independent convenience stores and small chains. It starts with staff-entered facts, owner-approved workflows, and clear evidence before any real store rollout.

REVIEW PILOT SCOPE
Never turned on here

No POS replacement, card processing, fuel control, lottery control, age-verification decision, face scanning, auto-ordering, employee discipline, payout, commission, or custody of money.

All Store Desk modules are OFF by default

Store Pulse

Shows exceptions first: blocked tasks, equipment incidents, receiving discrepancies, coverage gaps, aging vendor cases, and checklist failures.

Shift Command

Opening, mid-shift, closing, cleaning, safety, restock, and handoff playbooks with evidence and owner controls.

Asset Passport

Coolers, freezers, ice machines, registers, pumps, and selected equipment with incident, repair, proof, and recovery history.

Receiving Reality

Expected vs. received, shortage, damage, wrong item, invoice reference, evidence, and vendor follow-up without POS or ordering control.

Pattern Board

Truthful patterns from recorded facts only, such as repeated incidents or aging issues. No causation, fault, fraud, or certainty claims.

Store Network

Owner-approved, minimum-disclosure requests for repair/vendor recommendations, stock offers, coverage requests, and playbook exchange.

Activation rule

Build maximum capability. Activate only what passes.

  • Owner, manager, worker, and vendor access stays role-scoped.
  • Each company/store remains a private tenant.
  • AI is advisory only and cannot act without authorized human approval.
  • Future integrations begin in read-only synthetic shadow mode.
  • No fake health, completion, sensor, vendor, stock, compliance, or real-time claims.
